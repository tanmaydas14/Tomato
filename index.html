Important food-safety note

This is a school-project prototype. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe diets, calculate medical nutrition, or certify foods as safe. Allergens can be present through shared equipment or cross-contact. Always confirm with restaurant staff, especially for severe allergies. People managing diabetes or another condition should follow guidance from their qualified healthcare professional.
